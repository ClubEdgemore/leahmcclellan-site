What changed

The finish line moved.

A PM used to stop at the document. Now I can carry an idea all the way to something people can open, use, and react to, and still run it like a product.

Fuller concepts

Instead of handing off a PRD and a wireframe, I can show a working concept with real data and real interactions, so stakeholders and users react to the product, not a description of it.

Functional work

I build and ship with AI-assisted development. I know the systems & process well enough to QA it, troubleshoot it, and maintain it. I map the architecture so future updates stay easy.

Same PM rigor

Backlogs, user feedback, analytics, and KPIs still decide what gets built. AI speeds up the building. Judgment decides what’s worth building. Plus, with my background in marketing, I make things people want to use.

Product 01 · Mobile-first web app

Saturday is for CFB

A personalized college football web app for the whole football weekend.

Open Saturday is for CFB →
What shipped

Live scores, schedules, news, TV information, predictions, and team data, brought together in a personalized “football weekend” experience. I iterated the information architecture and UX on what I saw people needing and what they told me.

Built to be shared and kept

It installs to a phone’s home screen like an app. “Send My Schedule” lets fans text or email a link to what they’re watching this week. In-app feedback comes straight to me.

0-to-1 launch

About 1,000 users found it organically in the first week. I wrote the initial backlog, then used early-user feedback and surveys to prioritize features and refine the roadmap.

Data-informed

Product analytics show which teams people follow and how many they add. That behavior informs product decisions and points to monetization opportunities.

Product 02 · Accounts and catalog

Alibi Murder Mysteries

Live murder mystery parties you can host from your phone. Everyone has a secret. Everyone needs an alibi.

Open Alibi Murder Mysteries →
The experience

A host picks a mystery, sends each guest their character, and runs the whole night from their phone. Or they print everything for the table.

A real catalog

One free mystery to start, then paid mysteries from $14.99 to $29.99 across easy and medium difficulty. Each lists its suspect count and run time so hosts can match a mystery to their party.

Built for growth

The free mystery leads into account signup. Upcoming titles collect interest through email sign-ups, and hosts can request a custom mystery.

How I work

Discover, define, deliver, measure. Now with a build step in the loop.

01

Discover

User interviews, DScout studies, and behavioral data. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and NotebookLM help me synthesize insights and weigh alternatives faster.

02

Define

MoSCoW, level of effort, and quick wins turn a data-driven backlog into a roadmap. PRDs and user stories make the scope clear.

03

Build & deliver

Scrum sprint planning against team capacity and product goals. And when a working concept beats a document, I build it myself with AI.

04

Measure

Tableau, Adobe Analytics, and A/B tests track CRO, CTR, and impressions. Every launch gets measured against its KPIs.
